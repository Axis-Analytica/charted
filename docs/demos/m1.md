# Milestone 1 checkpoint: the Data view

Run `bin/checkpoint m1`. It builds charted, creates a fresh sample at
`~/Work/charted-checkpoints/Harbor & Pine` and (once, about a minute) a 10-million-row
one at `~/Work/charted-checkpoints/Harbor & Pine 10M`, saves screenshots to
`~/Work/charted-checkpoints/m1/`, and opens the app with the small sample.

1. **Speed.** Close the project (`Ctrl+W`) and open `Harbor & Pine 10M`, or run
   `target/debug/charted open "$HOME/Work/charted-checkpoints/Harbor & Pine 10M"`. Press `Ctrl+Alt+P` for the
   frame readout: *first rows* is the time from opening the table to painted rows
   (target: under 300 ms). Fling the scroll wheel and drag the scrollbar to the
   middle and the end: rows fill in, the readout stays near 60 fps, and `>33 ms`
   stays low.
2. **Select a column.** Click a header, or expand Sales in the sidebar and click a
   column. The properties panel opens on the right; the status bar shows the
   column's distinct values.
3. **Sort.** Hover a header and click its chevron (or press `Alt+↓` with a column
   selected): *Sort descending* on Quantity. Then Shift-click the UnitPrice header
   (or hold Shift when choosing *Sort ascending*) to add a second key. Headers show
   arrows with their priority; chips above the grid list the sort and remove it.
   On the 10M table a new sort takes a few hundred milliseconds, once; scrolling the
   sorted rows is as fast as unsorted.
4. **Filter.** On Channel, open the menu: the value list shows each value with its
   count. Untick *(Select all)*, tick Online, Apply. The header gets a funnel, the
   subtitle and status bar show "x of y rows", and the chip opens the menu again.
   Try *Condition* on Quantity: *is at least* 3. An invalid value ("lots") explains
   itself. `Ctrl+Shift+L` (or *Clear filters*) removes all filters.
5. **Statistics.** Select UnitPrice and press `Ctrl+I`: count, distinct, blanks,
   zeros, min/max, mean, median, deviation, quartiles and a histogram (hover a bar),
   all for the filtered rows. Text columns show their most common values; dates show
   their span.
6. **Format and undo.** In the properties panel change UnitPrice's format to
   *Percentage*, or type `#,0.0` in *Format string*: the preview updates as you type
   and the grid on Enter. `Ctrl+Z` undoes it; `Ctrl+S` saves it to
   `model.ossie.yaml`. Rename a column, change summarize-by, data category or
   hidden: each is one undo step.
7. **Checks against the data.** Set Store → City to *Whole number*: refused, with an
   example value. Set Date → Weekday's *Sort by column* to MonthNumber: refused
   (not one-to-one). MonthName is already sorted by MonthNumber, so sorting
   MonthName puts January first, not April.
8. **Copy.** Select cells (or click a header for the whole column, Ctrl-click for
   more) and press `Ctrl+C`; paste into a spreadsheet: tab-separated, formatted as
   shown, with headers for multi-row selections (up to a million rows).
9. **Find.** `Ctrl+F`, type `north`, Enter / Shift+Enter: jumps between matches in
   view order (sorted and filtered), wrapping at the end.
10. **Languages.** As in M0, `echo da > ~/.config/omarchy/language`: menus, the
    statistics and number separators switch to Danish.
