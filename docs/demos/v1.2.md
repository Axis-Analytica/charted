# v1.2 demo: visuals

New chart types, Top N filters everywhere, conditional formatting, analytics lines, small
multiples and report-page tooltips. Everything here is built from the sample (*Explore
the sample* on the welcome screen); `cd ui && CHARTED_SHOTS=<dir> npx playwright test
e2e/v12.spec.ts` runs the same flows and saves screenshots (`v12-*.png`).

1. **100% stacked column and bar.** Add a page, pick *100% stacked column chart* (the
   gallery's search finds it as *100%*, *percent* or *share*), tick Product → Category
   and Sales → Total Sales, then drag Store → Channel into *Legend*. Every column is
   100% tall, split by channel; hovering shows each channel's sales and its share
   ("$9,120,445 (63.8%)"). The bar version is next to it.
2. **Line and column combo.** Pick *Line and clustered column chart*, tick Date → Year,
   Total Sales and Units: Total Sales becomes the columns (*Column y-axis*) and Units
   the line (*Line y-axis*) on a secondary axis on the right (Format → *Secondary
   y-axis* turns it off, sharing one axis). Drag Channel into *Column legend*: the
   columns split by channel, and the line stays the year's units. Pick *Line and
   stacked column chart* in the gallery to stack the columns; the fields stay where
   they were.
3. **Waterfall.** *Waterfall chart* with Year and Total Sales: each year is a step up
   (green) or down (red) from the running total, with a grey Total at the end. Drag
   Channel into *Breakdown*: each year's total is followed by how each channel changed
   from the year before (the largest five, the rest as *Other*; Format → *Breakdown*).
   Format → *Sentiment colors* sets the three colours.
4. **Funnel and treemap.** *Funnel* with Category and Total Sales shows the categories
   widest first, each labelled with its share of the first. *Treemap* with the same
   fields tiles them by size; drag Subcategory into *Details* to split each category.
   Clicking a stage or tile filters the rest of the page, and right-click drills through.
5. **Gauge and KPI.** *Gauge* with Total Sales, and Targets → Target in *Target value*:
   the arc fills to the value, with a mark at the target (without a maximum the arc
   runs to twice the value; Format → *Gauge axis* sets min, max and target by hand).
   *KPI* with Total Sales (*Value*), Year (*Trend axis*) and Target: the latest year's
   sales in green when on target (red when not; *Color coding* → *Low is good* flips
   it), the distance to the target ("Target: $8.94M (+53.7%)") and the trend behind.
6. **Multi-row card.** *Multi-row card* with Total Sales, Units and Orders shows three
   callouts side by side, each with its label (Format → *Layout* sets columns and the
   accent bar). Power BI's multi-row card and new card import as this.
7. **Top N on a page or the report.** With nothing selected, drag Product → Category
   into *Filters on this page*, open it, *Filter type* → *Top N*, Top 3 by Total Sales,
   *Apply filter*: every visual on the page shows only the three biggest categories.
   The same works in *Filters on all pages* and on a visual; *By value* lists measures
   and summarised numeric columns.
8. **Conditional formatting.** A table of Category and Total Sales; Format → *Cell
   elements* lists each value column with *Background color*, *Font color*, *Data bars*
   and *Icons*. *Background color* opens the dialog: a gradient from the lowest to the
   highest value (add a middle colour, or set numbers), or *Rules* ("If value ≥ 1000000
   and < 5000000 then …", as numbers or percents of the range). *What field should we
   base this on?* can be any measure, even one not in the table: *Icons* based on
   Units puts an arrow by each row. *Data bars* draws a bar behind each value. Matrices
   format their value cells the same way (not the totals).
9. **Analytics lines.** A column chart of Category and Total Sales; Format → *Analytics*:
   *Average line*, *Min line*, *Max line*, *Trend line* (a least-squares fit) and a
   *Constant line* at a value you type, each labelled. Line, bar, stacked and combo
   charts have them too.
10. **Small multiples.** Drag Store → Region into the chart's *Small multiples* well: one
    small chart per region in a grid, sharing the y-axis (Format → *Small multiples*
    sets the columns, or lets each chart scale on its own). Clicking a bar chooses that
    category in that region.
11. **Report-page tooltips.** Add a page *Category tip*; with nothing selected, Format →
    *Use as a tooltip*: it becomes 320 × 240 and its tab gets a tooltip mark. Put a card
    of Total Sales on it and drag Category into *Tooltip fields* in the Filters pane.
    Back on a chart by Category, hovering a bar shows that page, filtered to the bar's
    category (and the page's filters and slicers). Format → *Tooltips* on the chart picks
    a page by hand, *Automatic* (the default: the first tooltip page whose tooltip fields
    the chart has) or *Default tooltip*. Column, bar, line, combo, pie and donut charts
    use them.
12. **Power BI round trip.** File → *Export Power BI project…*: the new charts are written
    as `hundredPercentStackedColumnChart`, `lineClusteredColumnComboChart` (columns in
    *Y*, the line in *Y2*), `waterfallChart`, `funnel`, `treemap`, `gauge`, `kpi` and
    `multiRowCard`, small multiples in *Rows*, and tooltip pages as Power BI tooltip pages
    (hidden in view mode). Importing the export brings everything back exactly.
